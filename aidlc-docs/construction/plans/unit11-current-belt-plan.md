## Deviations

- `BjjRules.CssName(Belt)` was kept rather than renamed to `BeltName`: the brief's list of files to change does not include `Road.razor` or `BeltBand.razor` (the two existing `CssName` callers), so renaming it would have touched files outside the authorized set. `BeltName(Belt)` was added as a new public method delegating to `CssName`, giving the current-belt feature (RankBar, SiteContentService, the admin select's messages, `ValidateDegreesAgainstEras`/`ValidateCurrentBeltAgainstEras`) its own clearly-named entry point without duplicating the closed-set mapping or touching the road's rendering. **Resolved in commit `a08bd48`** (post-merge remediation): `BeltName` is now the single implementation — the switch and its XML summary moved onto it — `CssName` was deleted, and `Road.razor`/`BeltBand.razor` were updated to call `BjjRules.BeltName` directly; a repo-wide grep for `CssName` in `src` and `tests` now returns zero hits.
- "reusing the era parser's mapping... use them from the era parser and validator" was read as: `TryParseBelt` reuses `ParseBelt`'s existing mapping (delegates to it) rather than restating the switch, and `BeltName` is used inside the (eras-comparing) validator `ValidateDegreesAgainstEras` to name the belt in its message. `ParseEras` and `ValidateEras` themselves were left calling `ParseBelt` exactly as before — the brief's own `SiteConfig` bullet keeps `BjjRules.ParseBelt(config["SITE_CURRENT_BELT"])` as the env parse, confirming `ParseBelt` stays the canonical nullable-returning parse.
- `SiteContentRules.CurrentBeltMaxLength` landed in the "resolve and validate paths" commit rather than "the column and migration" commit: it lives in `SiteContentRules.cs`, the same file as the rest of that commit's diff, and git stages whole files rather than partial diffs, so splitting it out would have needed interactive patching (not used on this machine).
- `.env.example`'s existing `SITE_BELT_DEGREES` comment gained "(degrees on a black belt)" alongside the admin hint's identical new wording (FR-C4) — a small doc-consistency addition beyond the brief's literal ask, not requested but harmless and accurate.
- Render-and-look opened purple (both themes, both widths), white (both themes, the light-mode ring confirmed via computed style — `--border` `#d8d1c2` distinct from both `--rank-white` `#e6dfd0` and the light page background `#f6f3ec`) and black (red bar, correct stripe count) live in the browser; blue and brown were not separately opened — they share purple's exact mechanism and are already pinned per-belt by `AppCssTests` and `LandingSectionsRenderTests`. Noted rather than spending the full time budget cycling five near-identical restarts.
- The app's actual port during the render check was `5072` (the project's `launchSettings.json` `applicationUrl`, which `dotnet run` applies after environment variables), not the `5199` the brief's example exports — harmless; the app came up correctly and every check ran against it.
