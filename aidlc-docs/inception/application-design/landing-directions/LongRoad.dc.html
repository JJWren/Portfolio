About

I am a passionate builder that loves seeing minor details sparkle and complex machina run smoothly and securely.

I have been building software applications for 6 years and have completed a BASc degree in Cyber Operations focusing on engineering. I currently work at Acentra Health where I build healthcare software that integrates care management, utilization management, and prior authorization requests into a single system. Before this, I worked in aviation for just over 10 years, 9 of which were spent directly in or in support of the US Army.

Outside of my professional life, I like to tinker with my home lab where I build and break various home and software automations, and I practice picking apart malware or investigating new / current security practices. I am a Brazilian jiu-jitsu black belt instructor with over 20 years of experience in the martial art and I love training as much as I can fit into my busy schedule. I have an amazing and supportive wife whom I raise 5 kids and 6 dogs with!
